# Final executive readout

| | |
|---|---|
| **Owner** | FDE or deployment lead |
| **When** | End of the pilot or engagement |
| **Inputs** | Measured results, the business case, risks |
| **Done when** | Leads with a recommendation and a measured number, and ends with a dated decision request |
| **Book** | Chapter 3, Section 3.7 |

1. **Recommendation, in one sentence, with a measured number.**
2. **Evidence:** how it was measured, on how many people, over what period, and the main assumption.
3. **What it's worth:** the business case re-run with measured values (status column kept), and its sensitivity.
4. **What could go wrong:** top three risks, each with an owner and a mitigation.
5. **Decision requested, with a date.**

One page. Method and data in an appendix. The first paragraph must survive being forwarded without context.
