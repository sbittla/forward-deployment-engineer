## Questions (45 minutes)
1. Walk me through the last time you handled <the task>. What did you do first? Where did you wait?
2. What does a good outcome look like? How would we know, in numbers, that it happened?
3. What is that number today, and who measures it?
4. Who uses the result of your work, and what do they do with it?
5. What data or systems do you use? Which do you trust, and which do you work around?
6. What must this system never do? (Compliance, safety, reputation.)
7. If two of the people involved disagree, who decides?
8. What would make you stop using it after a week?
9. Who else should I talk to? Who would be upset not to be asked?
