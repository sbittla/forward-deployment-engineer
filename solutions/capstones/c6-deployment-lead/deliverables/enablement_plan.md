# Enablement plan: Meridian Bank briefing assistant

## Audiences
| Audience | Size | Goal |
|---|---|---|
| Relationship managers (commercial, private banking) | 120 | Use the assistant for every client briefing |
| Team leads | 12 | Coach usage, review quality, run champions |
| Bank IT operations | 6 | Operate, monitor and roll back without the FDE |

## Workshops
- RM hands-on session (90 min): live briefing on your own client, verify sources, report a bad answer.
- IT operations code-with session (half day): deploy, roll back, read dashboards, rotate secrets, run the eval suite.
- Team-lead session (60 min): reading the adoption dashboard and coaching.

## Champions
One named champion per 10 RMs. Champions get early builds, a weekly office hour with the FDE, and a direct line for
feedback that goes into the product backlog.

## Knowledge-transfer exit criteria
- At least two Bank IT engineers independently complete deploy, rollback and eval runs (no single points of failure).
- Runbook walkthrough signed off by the IT operations lead.
- Every escalation path has a named bank owner.

## Adoption targets
| Week after launch | Activation | Habitual use |
|---|---|---|
| 2 | 40% | 15% |
| 4 | 60% | 30% |
| 8 | 75% | 45% |

Private banking is lagging (25% activation in the pilot): sponsor message from the Head of Private Banking, a
dedicated workshop and two named champions before week 2.
