# Personal Auto Policy PAP-1

## Collision
Collision coverage pays to repair your car after an accident with another vehicle or object, minus the collision
deductible of $500.

## Comprehensive
Comprehensive coverage pays for theft, vandalism, fire, falling objects and animal strikes, minus a $250 deductible.
Glass repair for chips smaller than a dollar bill is covered with no deductible.

## Rental reimbursement
Rental car costs are reimbursed up to $40 per day for a maximum of 30 days while your car is being repaired after a
covered claim.

## Roadside assistance
Towing up to 15 miles, jump starts, lockout service and flat tire changes are included at no extra cost.
