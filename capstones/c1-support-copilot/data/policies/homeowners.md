# Homeowners Policy HO-3

## Coverage
### Water damage
Sudden and accidental water damage from a burst pipe or appliance leak is covered up to the dwelling limit.
Gradual leaks and seepage over weeks or months are not covered.

### Flood
Flood damage from rising surface water, storm surge or overflowing rivers is excluded. Flood protection requires a
separate flood policy from the National Flood Insurance Program or a private carrier.

### Wind and hail
Wind and hail damage to the roof and siding is covered. A separate wind and hail deductible of 2% of the dwelling
limit applies in coastal counties.

## Deductibles
The standard all-perils deductible is $1,000 per claim. You can lower it to $500 for an additional premium.

## Personal property
Personal property is covered at actual cash value unless you add the replacement cost endorsement.
Jewelry theft is limited to $1,500 unless items are scheduled.

## Additional living expenses
If your home is uninhabitable after a covered loss, hotel and meal costs are covered for up to 12 months.
