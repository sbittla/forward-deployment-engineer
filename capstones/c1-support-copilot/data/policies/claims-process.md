# Claims Process Guide

## Filing a claim
Claims must be filed within 30 days of the loss. File online, in the mobile app, or by calling the claims line.
Include photos of the damage, a list of damaged items and any police report number.

## Adjuster visit
An adjuster contacts you within 2 business days to schedule an inspection. Keep damaged items until the adjuster
has inspected them.

## Payment
Approved claims are paid by direct deposit within 5 business days after the adjuster's final report.
Payments for mortgaged homes are issued jointly to you and your mortgage company.

## Appeals
If you disagree with a claim decision, you can request an appeal in writing within 60 days. A different adjuster
reviews every appeal.
